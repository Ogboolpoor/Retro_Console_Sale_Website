# Retro Console Sales Platform

A dedicated website and community hub for selling retro gaming consoles. It serves as both an e-commerce store and a blog, facilitating communication between the site administrators and retro gaming enthusiasts through articles, videos, and comments.

## Plugins Used

* **WooCommerce:** Powers the e-commerce functionality, product listings, and checkout process.
* **Members:** Manages user roles, permissions, and account access.
* **Embed Plus for YouTube Gallery:** seamlessly integrates and displays YouTube video content directly on the site.

## Features

- **E-Commerce Shop:** Browse, view, and purchase a variety of retro consoles and related products.
- **User Accounts:** Full registration, login, and account management system for visitors and buyers.
- **Blog & Articles:** Read up-to-date posts and articles, browse by tags, and engage with the community by leaving comments.
- **Video Galleries:** Watch embedded YouTube videos related to retro gaming directly on the platform.
- **Admin Portal:** Dedicated backend tools for administrators to easily create posts, manage tags, and oversee site interactions.
